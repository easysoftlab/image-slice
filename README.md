## English

### Overview

**Picture Slice (Yuheng Picture Slice)** is a locally running image slicing tool that splits single or batch images into multiple pieces by equal rows/columns, fixed pixel dimensions or manually dragged guide lines. It features real-time preview, draggable split lines, template saving, various zoom levels and automatic generation of seamless stitching HTML code. The software runs entirely offline to protect the privacy and confidentiality of your photos.

At just $0.99 — far less than half a cup of coffee — this app is a truly capable tool that dramatically boosts your efficiency. No matter how you do the math, it's a steal. And there are zero strings attached: this is the full-featured version, and we guarantee no hidden fees, ever.

### ✨ Key Features

- **Three Split Modes** — Average split by rows/columns, fixed-size tiling by exact pixel dimensions, or manual draggable guide lines.
- **Draggable Split Lines** — Click to select a split line, then drag to adjust its position visually on the canvas.
- **Real-Time Preview** — Split lines and effects are displayed live on the canvas; preview and export share the same rendering engine.
- **Split Templates** — Save your current slice settings for reuse, rename or delete them, stored locally on your device.
- **Batch Processing** — Slice multiple images at once by average split, fixed size or template, with custom direction and view modes.
- **View Controls** — Zoom in, zoom out, actual size and fit-to-window for precise editing.
- **6 Output Formats** — Export to PNG, JPG, GIF, BMP, WebP, TIFF with multi-format selection.
- **Auto-Generate Seamless HTML** — Automatically assemble slices into a seamless browser-displayable stitching page.
- **Smart Naming & ZIP Packaging** — Custom filename prefix, original name + index or original name + size naming rules, optional ZIP packaging.
- **Flexible Direction** — Horizontal or vertical slicing, left-to-right or right-to-left start direction.
- **100% Offline & Private** — All slicing happens locally on your device. No images are ever uploaded to any server.
- **Multi-Language Interface** — Available in multiple languages for global users.
